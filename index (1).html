SHEET 01 — THE GAP
Why IGNITE exists

The same gap, found twice — independently.

Three industry-side surveys and one student-side survey, run more than a year apart with different methods, all point to the same conclusion: the practical-skills gap is real, both sides want to close it, and no structured channel exists to do so.

Theme Peenya Survey (n=4) ME Landscape, Pan-India (n=13) ASME PULSE — Students (n=22)
Fresh-graduate skills gap 75% rate it "Extremely" challenging — the sharpest hiring pain point Top-cited future challenge (38.5%, rising to 46.2% with joint mentions); corroborated at 3.46/5 severity Lack of certifications/skills is the #1 self-reported barrier (45.5%)
Collaboration appetite 67% highly willing to work with academic institutions Real appetite confirmed; sequencing is the limiting factor 77.3% actively seeking a structured internship route
Preferred focus CAD/CAM & problem-solving rated highest-value skills "Fundamentals beat tools" — a consistent cross-cutting finding 36.4% specifically request CAD / technical workshops
What's missing isn't willingness — it's a mechanism. Both sides are, on the evidence, asking for the same thing. The gap is structural: nothing currently converts that mutual willingness into a repeatable process. That's the exact problem IGNITE is built to solve.

SHEET 04 — MECHANISM
How it works

Three operating principles. One repeating loop.

IGNITE isn't an events calendar — it's a structured pipeline designed to run every semester, automatically.

01

Build the Database

Branch representatives and the core team research, verify, and build a domain-specific company list — with the IMTEX-sourced database as its foundation.

OUTPUT → A verified, domain-specific company database, ready for outreach
02

Engage & Gather Data

Structured outreach — emails, calls, physical visits — collects internship slots, skill requirements, and project domains. A simple, non-binding Letter of Intent is signed.

OUTPUT → A live internship-intelligence database, updated every semester
03

Analyse, Guide & Reshape

The data is read for trends — which domains are hiring, which skills matter — and turned into orientation sessions, mentorship, and direct curriculum feedback to faculty.

OUTPUT → Career roadmaps for students, feedback loops for faculty
The repeating loop: research feeds the club → the club generates new data → new data updates the research → faculty receives the findings → curriculum improves → students arrive better prepared → industry gets better talent → industry engages more willingly. Every semester, on its own.
SHEET 05 — EXCHANGE
The industry engagement framework

An equal exchange — never a favour asked.

Developed from direct field observations, including the TVS Mysore plant visit and the Peenya survey visits.

We give you access to motivated, multi-branch student talent and share industry research with you — all we ask is 20–30 minutes of your perspective.

What IGNITE Asks

  • 20–30 minutes of time for one structured visit — never exceeded
  • General hiring & skills insights — no confidential operational data
  • Internship or project slots, entirely as per the company's own capacity
  • A simple, one-page, non-binding Letter of Intent

What Industry Gets

  • A free, pre-screened intern pipeline across every branch
  • The full industry benchmark report, shared at no cost
  • Real, pending problems handed to student project teams
  • First access to talent, ahead of the open placement market

SHEET 06 — ROADMAP
Proven human model, first

Three phases. The platform comes last, on purpose.

An app with no validated engagement fails quietly. IGNITE proves the corridor works with people first — then builds the digital layer on top of what already works.

Phase 1 — Now

Human Corridor

The IGNITE club itself: fieldwork, company visits, and direct student–industry connection — pending formal faculty approval.

Phase 2 — Within 6 months

Intelligence System

A structured internship database with semester-wise updates, built on the outreach process already proven in Survey 3.

Phase 3 — After Phase 2 is proven

Digital Platform

A web portal where companies self-update internship slots and students apply directly — built by the CSE department as a live student project, at zero cost.

      SHEET 09 — INITIATOR
      Behind IGNITE

      One student, four surveys, a year of fieldwork.

      SG

      Sai Gandhi V

      Student Initiator, IGNITE · Technical Officer, ASME Student Chapter · 4th Year B.Tech Mechanical Engineering, MSRUAS

      IGNITE began with a single question in the 4th semester: what do Bengaluru's mechanical-engineering companies actually need from a fresh graduate, and where does the curriculum fall short? That question turned into four independent surveys, a self-built outreach system, and a 550+-contact industry database — compiled solo, alongside a full course load.

      When the original Peenya survey's digital files were lost, the report was rebuilt from a retained hard copy — preserving all four verified responses without alteration, rather than treating the data as gone. That same persistence carried the research from a single city to a six-hub, five-state study, and from a proposal idea to a departmental Letter of Appreciation.
